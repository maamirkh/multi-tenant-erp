# DevSphere ERP

## Official Product Roadmap

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

---

# Purpose

This document defines the long-term product roadmap for DevSphere ERP.

It serves as the strategic plan for future development, helping ensure that every release contributes toward a unified enterprise platform.

The roadmap is divided into multiple versions, each introducing new capabilities while maintaining stability and scalability.

---

# Product Vision

Build a modern, AI-powered, multi-tenant ERP platform that enables businesses of every size to manage their complete operations from a single system.

Long-term goals include:

* Multi-Company Support
* Multi-Branch Support
* AI Automation
* Business Intelligence
* Mobile Applications
* Enterprise SaaS
* Global Expansion

---

# Guiding Principles

Every roadmap decision should prioritize:

* Stability
* Scalability
* Security
* Simplicity
* Automation
* Excellent User Experience
